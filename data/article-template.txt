How to publish an article

Open data/articles.json.

Copy this object, paste it at the top of the array after the opening [, then add a comma after it if another article follows.

{
  "slug": "short-url-name-with-dashes",
  "title": "Article headline",
  "date": "2026-05-17",
  "dek": "One or two sentence summary that appears on the homepage and article list.",
  "author": "Senate Probability Desk",
  "featured": true,
  "body": [
    "First paragraph.",
    "Second paragraph.",
    "Third paragraph."
  ]
}

Rules:
- Keep slug lowercase with dashes only.
- Only one article needs featured: true. The newest featured article appears on the homepage.
- Use straight quotes, not curly quotes, inside JSON.
- Each paragraph in body must be in quotes and separated by commas.
- Run `node -e "JSON.parse(require('fs').readFileSync('data/articles.json','utf8')); console.log('articles ok')"` if you want to check the file before pushing.
