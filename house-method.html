House

Method.

Inputs
Ratings
Cook public district ratings for the full 435-seat board; Inside Elections via 270toWin as a secondary check where available.
Polling
Generic-ballot blend from the same public adapters used by the Senate model. House race-poll pages are monitored but not force-blended without stable district rows.
Districts
Open seats, rating bucket error, state-level correlation, national error, and the current 119th district map through district-level ratings and margins.
Map basis
Current display
435-district cartogram.
Boundary source
Census 2025 cartographic boundary files for the 119th congressional districts.
Shape map
Not bundled yet; the cartogram is used until a local district GeoJSON is added.
Redistricting
Handled through the individual district inputs; no extra top-line party bonus is added after the seats are modeled.
